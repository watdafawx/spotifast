Spotifast (mods) bundles two programs beside spotifast.exe for the music
video behind full-screen lyrics. Spotifast loads or runs them unchanged;
they are not part of Spotifast and keep their own licences.

libmpv-2.dll
  mpv's library, from shinchiro's mpv-winbuild-cmake build
  mpv-dev-x86_64-20261101-git-48fca90e8c (mpv commit 48fca90e8c).
  mpv is licensed under the GNU GPL version 2 or later, or the GNU LGPL
  version 2.1 or later when built without its GPL parts. This build
  includes GPL libraries (x264, x265, and others), so it is distributed
  under the GNU GPL. Source code:
    https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv/tree/48fca90e8c
    https://github.com/shinchiro/mpv-winbuild-cmake (the build scripts
    and the sources of every bundled library)
  Licence texts: https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv/blob/master/Copyright

yt-dlp.exe
  yt-dlp 2026.10.26, from https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/releases
  Released into the public domain under the Unlicense:
    https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/blob/master/LICENSE
  The executable bundles Python and other components under their own
  licences, listed in its THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt:
    https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/blob/master/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt

YouTube changes often, and an old yt-dlp then stops finding videos. To use
a newer one, put its yt-dlp.exe in this folder in place of the bundled one,
or run "yt-dlp.exe -U" here, and restart Spotifast. Updating Spotifast
keeps a yt-dlp.exe that is newer than the one it brings.
